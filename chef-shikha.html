Meet Chef Shikha

As long as I remember I have loved big family dinners. Food can be a great way to bond with family and friends. It can also be an escape into something exotic and new.

Before food, I spent over ten years in television, producing the cooking shows "Sunny Side Up" and "Pure Sin" and directing "Unexplored Goa" for National Geographic. In 2016 my hunger for cooking took me to Paris, to train as a chef at Ferrandi and to work at Kokoro. In 2018 I spent three months at Fäviken in Sweden, foraging, preserving and cooking.

In 2017 I started Myhomebistro so everyone can experience fine dining in their own home. I believe the most exquisite tastes come from local and seasonal produce, so one ingredient is always the star.
