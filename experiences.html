Experiences

Five ways to bring Chef Shikha's cooking to your people.

Private dining at home

Our heart. A sit-down meal of three to five courses, opening with an amuse-bouche, cooked and served in your home for 2 to 50 guests. We bring the table linen, cutlery, glasses and plates, and Chef Shikha introduces every course.

Celebrations and events

Birthdays, anniversaries and gatherings up to 50 guests. Chef Shikha brings a team for the final cooking, plating and service.

Festive menus

Set menus for the seasons: Christmas, New Year and Ganpati specials.

Delivery and bakes

Delivered menus and baked goods, including our loaf cakes.

For brands and spaces

Food partnerships and consulting. We cook daily food and weekly loaf cakes for Sloth Society in Bandra, and have cooked live on Canon's stand at a broadcast exhibition.
